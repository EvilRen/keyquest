Missions

Find the glowing key on screen, press it on the real keyboard. Right key, you strike. Wrong key, the enemy strikes back — lose all your hearts and the mission ends. Clear a mission without a single mistake for three stars.

Voice pack

The computer voice is robotic. Record your own instead: tap a key to record about a second and a half, tap it again later to hear it. Anything you record is used in the game; anything you skip falls back to the computer voice.

Free practice

Type any word — a name, a password — and the keys will light up for it.
